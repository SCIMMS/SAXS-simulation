# Figure S19: aspect sensitivity

Five-plane refinement with nested aspect bounds. All predetermined ranges and 209 frames are retained. Effective-radius changes are assessed together with predictive losses and aspect/scale bounds; no range is chosen by its test score. This follow-up was developed after earlier held-out results were viewed and is exploratory within-pattern validation, not a new independent confirmatory cohort.
