# Table S9

Table S9 Physical projected-ellipse comparison. Each model mean is the equal-frame mean of its correlation scores over 209 frames. Mean difference is the equal-frame mean of core-shell minus ellipse scores for the same evaluation pixels; positive favors core-shell. Displayed means and differences are rounded independently. Scores are log-intensity correlations, not the later log-MSE refinement objective. Parameter bounds and incomplete local convergence are retained in the complete source CSV.

`data/table.csv` transcribes the final R18 native Word table, preserving displayed rounding, units and footnote symbols. Unrounded measurements and conditions are in the [validation package](../../../../validation_package/revision_2026_10_17/README.md).
