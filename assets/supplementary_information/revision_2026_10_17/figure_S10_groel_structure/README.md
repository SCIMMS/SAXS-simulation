# Figure S10: groel structure

Structural input used for GroEL density-model construction. C-alpha coordinates from public PDB structure 1SS8 are shown for the original seven-subunit ring A (C7 heptamer, with sevenfold cyclic symmetry) and the copy generated by the PDB biological-assembly transformation records (BIOMT), used to construct the two-ring B model (D7 tetradecamer, with dihedral symmetry). The left panel overlays the original heptamer and BIOMT copy in an x-z view, and the middle and right panels show orthogonal x-y and y-z projections of the tetradecamer preview. Chain-colored traces identify the original heptamer, while the semi-transparent brown points indicate the BIOMT-related copy. This panel documents the structural origin of the PDB-derived density model only; it is not an atomistic refinement, orientation assignment, or molecular-dynamics result.
