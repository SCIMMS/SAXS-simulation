# Figure S6: id57 planar residuals

Historical planar residual diagnosis for the original ID57 illustrations, frames 0 and 1, after the rounded-library comparison. Measured and modeled log1p intensities use a shared scale and residuals share a symmetric range. Fine interference and an additional streak remain unassigned to a specific physical cause; these frames are not presented as clean single-hit validation. Section S4.2 describes a restricted coherent-pair diagnostic, and Section S7 assesses illumination and background considerations. Main Figure 5 shows alternative frames and their projected model densities.
