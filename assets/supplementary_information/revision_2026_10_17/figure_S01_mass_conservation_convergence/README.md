# Figure S1: mass conservation convergence

Projected-mass conservation and resolution convergence. (a) Maximum absolute relative projected-mass error across the orientation sweep for the sphere, ellipsoid, box, and icosahedron, evaluated against the corresponding mesh volume. (b) Intensity relative L2 convergence for the sphere detector-resolution sweep and the ellipsoid mesh-resolution sweep. The trends separate mesh-geometry error from detector-plane sampling effects.
