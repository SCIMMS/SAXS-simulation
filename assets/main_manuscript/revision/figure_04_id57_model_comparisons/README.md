# Figure 4

ID57 model comparisons for all 209 frames. Each horizontal coordinate is a paired Δ log-correlation: the model log-correlation minus the comparator log-correlation for the same frame and evaluation pixels. (a) Historical planar structured-minus-radial-control comparison on the full region of interest (ROI). (b) Planar fixed core-shell minus fitted ellipsoid on complementary withheld radial-band folds. (c) Five-plane physical-Ewald rounded minus variable-size sharp comparisons after regenerating and reselecting the complete candidate libraries. In (b,c), even→odd and odd→even identify the parity of the radial bands used for training and evaluation, respectively; they do not identify frame parity. Geometry and nuisance parameters use the training bands and remain frozen on evaluation pixels. Positive differences favor the first model named in each panel. Panels show different matched comparisons; absolute scores across their numerical and scoring conventions are not treated as a common ranking. Complete results and direct parameter summaries are in Section S4.
