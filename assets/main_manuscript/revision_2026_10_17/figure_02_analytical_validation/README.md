# Figure 2: analytical validation

Analytical validation of the sample-level projected-density kernel. Rows show the sphere, rotated ellipsoid and exactly polyhedral box. Simulated intensities are compared with independently evaluated analytical references and residuals. Smooth-object comparisons include finite-mesh approximation; the box isolates the remaining numerical projection and transform errors. This figure validates the numerical kernel within the planar model, not the physical neglect of Ewald curvature.
