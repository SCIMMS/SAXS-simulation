# Figure 6: groel representative match

GroEL density-point/FFT comparison for selected pattern 67, source frame 30818, B tetradecamer template 21 at recorded rotation 2944. Panels show the measured intensity, fitted intensity, projected model density and log1p residual. Measured and fitted panels share the intensity scale; model density is in electrons Å⁻² and is not reconstructed from the shot. The recorded log-correlation is 0.7549, with a radial-control score 0.7400. The fit remains illustrative and retains unexplained residuals; it does not identify an assembly state. Figure S12 uses a different preview rotation and synthetic display normalization.
