IUCr-style reference list and citation-placement map

Note. IUCr/J. Synchrotron Rad. uses author-year citations in the manuscript text, and the reference list is arranged alphabetically. The numbers below are working numbers assigned to the alphabetized reference list so that citation placement can be planned consistently. In the final manuscript, use author-year citations such as (Neutze et al., 2000), not numeric citations. For references with three or more authors, "et al." remains appropriate in the manuscript text, but the reference list below gives the full author list.
