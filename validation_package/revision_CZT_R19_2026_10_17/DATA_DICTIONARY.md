# R19 definitions and records

`groel_mesh_czt_matched_2026-10-17` contains the frozen 2 x 2 point/mesh and FFT/CZT path comparison. `templates/template_NNN.npz` stores four routes in the explicit `routes` order, common rotation, point/mesh projections, extent in Å and template index. `fit_metrics.csv` retains 1,216 frame/route/fold records; `paired_twofold.csv` averages the two complementary differences within each frame before computing equal-frame means. `template_qa.csv`, `all_template_reference_audit.csv`, `numerical_controls.csv` and `independent_audit.json` retain numerical controls and diagnostics without exclusions.

`groel_czt_primary_2026-10-17` contains the primary CZT-only comparisons. `fixed_control_results.csv` contains 76 shots x 3 folds x 2 families = 456 records; `fixed_control_paired.csv` contains the 228 paired differences. `radial_permutation_audit.csv` checks all 34 controls. `ab_templates_mesh_czt.npz` contains A/B intensity predictions for the 201 views and their explicit indices; `ab_paired_results.csv` contains 456 assembly/fold records. The full-ROI `pattern67_primary.npz` and `pattern67_AB.npz` store the measured image, scored ROI and fitted predictions for the published examples. Figures S12–S14 and main Figure 6 are generated by `revision_groel_czt_public_figures.py figures`.

`pattern_index` is zero-based within the curated 76 shots; `source_frame_index` is the original run-frame identifier. Historical `template_index` addresses the 429-state B/R35/R55 seed grid. `rotation_index` addresses the recorded quaternion library, and differs from a candidate's position in the 201-view A/B array. Class, orientation and detector shifts are frozen in the primary fixed-assignment experiment. A/B is a separate view-selection experiment and does not revise those assignments.

RMS = sqrt(mean((prediction - observation)^2)) over the stated pixels, in pipeline-derived photon-count units. Signal and constant background are nonnegative and selected by least squares in linear intensity. Diagnostic log-correlation is Pearson correlation of log(1 + prediction) and log(1 + measured nonnegative intensity), evaluated at that fit. It is dimensionless and is not the fitting objective for GroEL. Lower RMS and higher correlation indicate greater agreement under this objective; they do not identify a protein assembly.

A paired difference uses the same shot and evaluation pixels. `paired_delta` is structured minus radial-control log-correlation; `RMS_delta` is structured minus control RMS. Paired mean/median summarizes these per-shot differences with equal shot weights. A/B differences use A minus B; the sign favoring A is negative for RMS. Separate marginal medians need not subtract to the paired median. Full-ROI evaluation reuses fitting pixels.

Primary path, fixed/control and A/B two-fold comparisons use radius r = hypot(x - 63.5, y - 63.5) in the 128 x 128 image and annulus 5 <= r <= 40. Band index is floor((r - 5)/5). `even_to_odd` fits even bands and evaluates odd bands; `odd_to_even` reverses them. Fitted nuisance coefficients and any training-selected view remain frozen on evaluation pixels. Class/orientation/alignment used the historical full ROI, so this is conditional within-image validation, not independent particle-shot validation.

Supplementary GroEL refinement in the R18 base uses a DIFFERENT stored split: band = floor(r/2), globally numbered from radius zero and restricted to the same ROI. Even indices train; indices 1 modulo 4 validate; indices 3 modulo 4 test. R19 corrects the earlier prose origin; no stored mask or fit changes. The primary paired control's 5-pixel origin must not be applied to these 2-pixel masks. ID57 uses its separately documented 10-pixel bands starting at radius 45 pixels.

All primary GroEL routes use transverse q in Å^-1 with q_z=0. Projection spacing is 1 Å; projected density is electrons Å^-2. The selected CZT lattice has spacing 2*pi/(2048 Å), preserving legacy half-pixel Fourier phase before bilinear complex-amplitude interpolation and modulus squared. K counts longitudinal-frequency samples; K=1 is the planar specialization. This release's planar boundary references do not validate physical Ewald curvature for GroEL.

Figure S12 uses log(1 + 100 I/Imax) with each model's own Imax for a synthetic preview, not an experimental shared intensity scale. Main Figure 6 and S14 instead share measured/fitted scales and use symmetric signed log1p residual limits. Density panels are forward projections of prescribed meshes, not reconstructed experimental densities.

The supplementary differentiable direct-boundary GroEL calculation remains a separate reference/extension in S6. Its residual normalization and regularization weights remain in the R18 source snapshots and current SI; the CZT primary table values must not be substituted into those exploratory refinement comparisons.
